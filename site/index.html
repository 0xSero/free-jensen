How it works

01

One person, one signature

Every signature passes a Cloudflare Turnstile human check. Each browser can sign once, and each network is capped at a few signatures a day, so the count can't be stuffed by bots or repeat clicks.

02

Counted one at a time

Each signature is a single atomic database write. The rate check, the signature, and the country tally all commit together or not at all, so no two signatures can collide or be lost. Counts update live for everyone within seconds.

03

Private by design

We never ask for your name, email, or phone number, and we never store your IP address. We keep only a one-way keyed hash of it for rate limiting. Your country comes from your network. Your optional comment stays private and is used only for aggregate sentiment analysis.
